Research

BodyFit

Can two silhouettes capture enough information to estimate body composition? I worked with paired front and side views, using separate image encoders to predict BMI and a body-fat estimate.

Method and evaluation

The pipeline extracts binary silhouettes and checks their quality before prediction. On an 80/20 split, validation mean absolute error was about 2.08 BMI units and 4.41 percentage points against body-fat proxy labels derived from the Body Adiposity Index.

BarçaIQ

A football analytics project that pairs structured match statistics with match-report retrieval to investigate tactical questions.

How it works

StatsBomb event data supplies the match statistics. SQL handles exact counts, ChromaDB retrieves relevant passages, and LangGraph coordinates research, analysis, and visualization.

AI playlist recommendation

A natural-language playlist builder that translates a listener’s mood and preferences into a ranked track list.

How it works

Llama translates the request into JSON. KMeans selects a pool of candidates, and cosine similarity ranks tracks using audio features such as energy, tempo, and danceability.
